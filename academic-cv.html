Undergraduate Thesis

Occurrence and Antimicrobial Susceptibility Profiles of Presumptive Salmonella Isolates from Fish and Fish-Processing Environments in Wet Markets of Sylhet City, Bangladesh

Defended 31 August 2026 | Food microbiology | AMR | One Health

Linked sampling covered 22 fish-processing events across 11 wet markets, seven matrices and 154 source samples. The workflow included XLD culture, biochemical characterization, attempted invA-targeted PCR confirmation and Kirby-Bauer antimicrobial susceptibility testing. Presumptive detection was 26.0% of source samples and 35.0% of retained isolates met the multidrug-resistance definition. Molecular confirmation was not achieved, so the findings remain presumptive rather than molecularly confirmed Salmonella.
