# P04 · Ecosystem services and livelihoods
Status: **specification**. Uses the P01 conventions: registration canvas, manifest IDs, evidence chips, state machine, reduced-motion and no-JS fallbacks. Evidence IDs resolve in `05-production-manifests/evidence.json`.

## P04-S01 · From water to household

| Field | Specification |
|---|---|
| Purpose | 60 species and household survey findings on livelihood dependence and cultural practice (P04-E001). |
| Visual | Layered landscape from water to homestead; people as silhouettes, no faces. Three registers: findings, authors’ interpretation, authors’ recommendations (quoted, not paraphrased). |
| Motion | Vertical reveal water → household (scroll-scrubbed, reversible); register cards rise once. |
| Interaction | Open each register; quotes show page references. |
| Boundary | Household counts and quotes must be taken from the paper (pending transcription). |
| Assets | Landscape layers (image generation, 4 depth layers, CS lighting morning 4500 K); silhouettes vector. |
