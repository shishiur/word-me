03 · Selective culture

Reading an XLD plate

On xylose lysine deoxycholate agar, Salmonella typically grows as red colonies with black centres: it ferments xylose, then decarboxylates lysine, which returns the medium to red, and its hydrogen sulphide turns the centre black.

Reference science

Choose a colony type to isolate it on the plate. Black centres mark hydrogen sulphide, which is why look-alikes such as Citrobacter and Proteus have to be removed by biochemical testing.

40 biochemically compatible isolates Researcher record

Biochemical tests remove look-alikes such as Citrobacter and Proteus, which also make black-centred colonies on XLD.

04 · Molecular confirmation Disputed

One gene, two accounts

invA sits in Salmonella pathogenicity island 1 and is a standard Salmonella-specific PCR target. Whether these 40 isolates carried it is the one fact this study’s conclusions turn on, and the researcher’s two sources disagree.

Researcher’s latest record provisional

All 40 retained isolates gave an invA PCR product, according to the researcher’s latest record. The Atlas uses this as a provisional working account until the gel photograph, controls and final thesis are checked.

05 · Antimicrobial susceptibility

Resistance on Mueller–Hinton

The 40 isolates (invA-positive isolates (provisional)) were each lawned onto Mueller–Hinton agar with antibiotic discs. A wide clear zone means the drug stopped growth; a narrow one means resistance. Twelve agents were tested and nine interpreted.

Multiple antibiotic resistance index

MAR divides the number of agents an isolate resists by the number tested. The study’s mean is 0.267 Researcher record. Krumperman (1983) proposed reading values above 0.2 as exposure from a high-risk source Reference science. Whether the denominator was 9 or 12 agents is still to be confirmed.

06 · Interpretation

What this study can claim

Under the Researcher’s latest record account.

Can claim

  • 40 isolates from 154 linked samples across 11 markets and 22 processing events, all invA PCR-positive (provisional until the gel and controls are checked).
  • Resistance phenotypes for those 40 isolates: ampicillin 24, tetracycline 20, multidrug resistance 14 (35.0%), mean MAR index 0.267.
  • Event-level detection for three matrices: fish skin 9 of 22, viscera-associated basket surface 8 of 22, cut flesh 3 of 22.

Cannot claim

  • That one matrix contaminated another. Co-occurrence at a station is not a measured transfer pathway.
  • Serovar, source attribution or clinical significance. No serotyping or molecular typing is in the record.
  • A sample-level occurrence percentage. The 26.0% on the old site equals 40 ÷ 154 and holds only if each isolate came from a different sample.
  • Independent verification. No P01 value has yet been checked against the thesis.
  • That the isolates are Salmonella. Molecular confirmation was not achieved in this account.
