P01 · Direction A

Tungsten Night

The investigation opens where the samples were taken: a cutter’s station before dawn, lit by one bare bulb. Every sampled surface is a lit object, and the visitor moves the light.

Selected: chapters 01–02

Type

Seven surfaces

one cutting station

154 SAMPLES · 22 EVENTS

Newsreader for scene titles, Instrument Sans for reading, IBM Plex Mono for counts and registers.

Material & light

One warm point source at upper left. Wet concrete carries long vertical reflections; the steel bowl and bonti take specular edges; bamboo and wood absorb. A 14% grain keeps it painted rather than photographic.

Motion

  • Bulb flicker: 1.6 s, stepped, once
  • Spotlight move: 550 ms ease-out
  • Leader emphasis: 300 ms
  • No ambient loops; all off under reduced motion

Interaction

  • Select a surface: 7 hotspots plus chips
  • Compare event-level detection bars
  • Selection carries into the ledger

Build

Inline SVG pre-rendered at build time (works without JavaScript). CSS mask for the spotlight, HTML buttons over the art for focus and screen readers.

Integrity safeguards

  • “Illustrative reconstruction” printed inside the art
  • Caption: positions are not a transmission pathway
  • Four inferred matrix names carry an asterisk

Evidence it uses

Researcher record design counts and three detection rates.

Strengths

  • Strongest first impression; grounds microbiology in a real place
  • Vernacular objects (bonti, bamboo basket, hanging balance) are specific to Bangladeshi wet markets
  • The spotlight is a precise, accessible selection device

Risks

  • Spatial staging can suggest transfer routes; mitigated by caption and no arrows between objects
  • A night scene can feel theatrical for a lab study
  • Needs a clear handover to the bench

P01 · Direction B

Lightbox Bench

Every one of the 154 samples is a plate on a laboratory lightbox. The story is attrition: plates lift into trays as the record narrows them from 154 to 62 to 40.

Selected: chapters 03–05

Type

154 → 62 → 40

the sieve narrows

UNORDERED TRAY · COUNT ONLY

Instrument Sans semi-bold for labels, Plex Mono for tray counts, Newsreader only for chapter titles.

Material & light

Cool, diffuse light from below. Glass rims, translucent agar, soft contact shadows. No environment at all.

Motion

  • Plates lift: 500 ms, staggered 8 ms each
  • Counts tick with the lift
  • Tray settles with a short ease-out

Interaction

  • Step through the sieve
  • Filter by matrix row or market column
  • Open a plate to read colony types

Build

SVG plates (Canvas above ~1,000 marks), FLIP transitions, the same evidence-tagged data module as A.

Integrity safeguards

  • Plates lift into an unordered tray, so no sample is shown as positive
  • Each tray carries its register chip
  • 62 is flagged until its unit (sample or colony) is confirmed

Evidence it uses

Researcher record 154 / 62 / 40 counts.

Strengths

  • Honest about attrition
  • Reads instantly as laboratory work
  • Scales to per-isolate data later

Risks

  • Clinical and repetitive across 154 identical plates
  • Loses the market context that carries the One Health argument
  • A lift metaphor implies identity unless the tray is clearly unordered

Tungsten Night carries chapters 01–02, where place and study design matter. Lightbox Bench carries chapters 03–05, where the bench is the honest setting. A gradient band hands one to the other.
