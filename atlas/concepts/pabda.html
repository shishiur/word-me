Pabda gut microbiology concept board

Two directions for the ongoing probiotic screening of gut-derived isolates from pabda. No results are public; the board shows methods, origin and the gates not yet reached.

Material & light

A translucent body with visible myomeres and vertebral line; warm lab light; a clean incision rim around the window.

Motion

  • Sample ring pulses once
  • Nothing moves inside the gut

Interaction

  • Open the window
  • Tap the sample site
  • Continue to the bench

Build

SVG fish with a clip-path window; the iris is a scaled clip path.

Integrity safeguards

  • “Not evidence of colonisation or benefit” printed in the art
  • No bacteria drawn on the gut wall
  • Species name withheld until 16S data

Evidence it uses

Researcher record project code and gut origin only.

Strengths

  • Answers “where did the isolates come from?” in one image
  • A second fish species gives the Atlas range
  • Calm and precise

Risks

  • Anatomical drama implies in vivo knowledge the project does not have
  • Tempts a later edit that draws bacteria lining the gut
  • Little to interact with

Stress Gauntlet

Gate 2 · bile salts

survival: awaiting counts

NOT YET TESTED

Plex Mono carries the bench; Newsreader only for the chapter title.

Material & light

Lab glass on a white bench under flat, diffuse light. Dashed outlines are the only “material” for gates not yet reached.

Motion

  • Isolate token moves gate to gate: 400 ms
  • Counts fade in only when raw data exist
  • Dashed gates never animate

Interaction

  • Pick an isolate once the list exists
  • Open a gate to read its protocol
  • Empty gates explain what each would test

Build

SVG bench; gate states from a JSON list of isolates when supplied.

Integrity safeguards

  • No counts invented; “awaiting raw counts” in place
  • Priestia/Bacillus report not shown until 16S data
  • Status banner: ongoing, researcher-supplied

Evidence it uses

Researcher record assays named in the brief; Awaiting evidence all values.

Strengths

  • Makes an unfinished project look rigorous rather than empty
  • The verb, “test”, is unique in the Atlas
  • Grows naturally as data arrive

Risks

  • Flat and diagrammatic beside P01 and P02
  • Many “awaiting” labels can read as a weak project
  • Gate order implies a fixed protocol sequence

Open in the gut, live on the bench.

From the Gut is the opening frame, with its warning printed in the art. The Stress Gauntlet is the experience. Until raw counts arrive, the bench shows methods and empty gates, which is accurate for an ongoing project.

Rejected: any bacteria drawn on the intestinal wall, and any species name before sequencing.
