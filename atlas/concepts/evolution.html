Research Evolution concept board

Two directions for showing how the research developed, built only from dated evidence. Published dates are filled glyphs; dates from the researcher are half-filled.

Evolution · Direction A

Braided Channels

Four method channels braid across 2023–2026, the way Sylhet’s rivers do. Projects sit where the evidence dates them; dotted crossings are claimed transfers of method.

Evidence it uses

Strengths

Risks

  • Label crowding around 2025–26
  • Weak on a phone without the list fallback

Braid the channels; order them by scale.

Braided Channels replaces the current eight-stage motivational path. Lanes run top to bottom from molecule to community, which is the one idea worth taking from the Scale Field.

The current site says the research “began with computational biology”. The dated record does not support that, so the braid starts with the 2023 hilsa sampling and runs the computational lane alongside it.
